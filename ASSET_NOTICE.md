its code-rendered interface, original SVG app icon, and recordings of that interface.

The supplied reference images in `docs/references/` are **not covered by Pulse's
MIT license**. Their original authors and redistribution terms have not been
established. They are excluded from source and wheel release packages; do not
redistribute them as MIT-licensed assets.

Third-party dependencies, desktop application icons, and artwork provided by
music players remain under their respective licenses. Pulse's license does not
transfer rights to those materials.
